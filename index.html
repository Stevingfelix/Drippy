Frequently Asked Questions About Global Logistics & Freight

Have questions about international cargo transport, customs clearance, or real-time shipment visibility? Here are clear answers to how Drippy manages global supply chains.

What international shipping methods and freight modes do you offer?
We provide comprehensive multimodal freight solutions worldwide, including Ocean Freight (FCL full container load and LCL consolidated cargo), Air Freight Express and Standard services, Rail Intermodal corridors, and temperature-controlled cold chain logistics. Every shipment is backed by live GPS and milestone tracking from origin port to final destination.
How does Drippy handle customs clearance and import/export documentation?
Our in-house licensed customs brokerage specialists oversee all documentation, tariff classifications (HS codes), import/export declarations, duty optimization, and regulatory compliance. We handle pre-arrival clearance at major sea and air terminals to eliminate dwell times and prevent costly port demurrage fees.
How can I track my shipment in real-time?
You can track any consignment directly on our Track & Trace portal using your Air Waybill (AWB), Bill of Lading (BOL), or Drippy container tracking number. Our platform provides live location coordinates, vessel status, customs milestone updates, estimated time of arrival (ETA), and automated email/SMS status notifications.
What cargo insurance options and risk coverage are available?
We offer comprehensive All-Risk Marine and Transit Cargo Insurance covering full invoice value plus freight costs from door-to-door. Coverage protects against physical loss, damage, natural disasters, and general average situations across ocean, air, and intermodal transport segments.
How quickly can I receive a freight quote for bulk or specialized cargo?
Standard freight quotes submitted via our online Request a Quote form receive competitive rates within 2–4 hours during business hours. For complex supply chain projects, out-of-gauge (OOG) machinery, or long-term contract logistics, our dedicated trade lane specialists prepare detailed routing schedules and pricing within 24 hours.
